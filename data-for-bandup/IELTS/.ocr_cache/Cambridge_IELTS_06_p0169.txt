A range of vocabulary is used with flexibility and a good level of control. There are examples
of appropriate idiomatic expressions that suggest that the writer has a good active resource.

communication.

sophisticated phrasing, However, there are quite a lot of short, simple sentences too, and there
are lapses and omissions in some structures, although these do not prevent the reader from
