Essay [ Candidate Essay] 
 
This is an answer written by a candidate who achieved a Band 6.0 score. The 
given diagram describes the process of manufacturing bamboo fabrics. Overall, 
the process includes a few main stages, which are showen on the diagram and 
are numbered from 1 to 8. 
The first stage, obviously, consists of planting and growing bamboo plants, 
usually happening in spring. 
The second phase, We could say harvesting one, is happening in the middle part 
of the year in autumn. Bamboo stems are cutting off by the season workers, 
collecting and sendening to the special plants during this period. 
In the plants, what are showed a the third and the forth stages. on the diagram, 
the bamboo stems are cutting into long strips, then produced strips are crushing 
to make liquid pulp. 
During the next phase these pulp is filtering through the special types of sieves 
to separate long fibres from liquid. 
Then the fibres, filtered by previous step, are being undergone through the 
softenning procedure. This procedure includes adding water and amine oxide. 
The seventh stage is the sppinning the soften fibres to make a strong bamboo 
thread. 
  
Produced yarns might be used to make fabric, which is the last - eighth phase, 
by weaveing them using special equipment.
