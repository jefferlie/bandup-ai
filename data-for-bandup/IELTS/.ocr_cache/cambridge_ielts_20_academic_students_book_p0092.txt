Research in these fields is being conducted in laboratories around the world, with 
the London Zoo centre linked to this global network. Studies carried out in one 
centre can then be tested in others. The resulting young coral can then be 
displayed in the tank in Tiny Giants. 'The crucial point is that the progress we make 
in making coral better able to survive in a warming world can be shown to the 
public and encourage them to believe that we can do something to save the 
planet's reefs,' said Pearce-Kelly. 'Saving our coral reefs is now a critically 
important ecological goal.'
