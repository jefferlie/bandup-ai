Model and sample answers for Writing tasks

TEST A, WRITING TASK 1 (GENERAL TRAINING)

MODEL ANSWER

This model has been prepared by an examiner as an example of a very good answer. However, please

note that this is just one example out of many possible approaches.
Dear Jenny,
| hope this finds you and your family well, and I’m sorry | haven't been in
touch recently. I’m writing now to let you know about my new job.
You remember that | was working for that construction company a few miles
outside the city? Well, | just got so fed up with it — | was working really long
hours and, to be honest, the pay wasn’t great. So basically | applied for a job
at a new international company that was looking for admin staff for a new
branch in the city centre.
| was a bit nervous in the interview, but in the end | got a job as one of the
senior administrators. | am so happy! The pay is better, the working
conditions are great and | don’t have to drive to work anymore.
Anyway, | have to stop now because my sister is staying with me and has
brought my baby nephew to meet me. She sends you lots of love and is
asking when we can all meet up. Soon, | hope.
With love,
Tina
