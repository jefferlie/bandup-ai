Well, I wouldn't say that having ambitions is always a positive thing. I mean, 
on one hand, ambition pushes people to grow, to achieve more, and to aim higher 
in life. It's definitely a driving force behind success. But on the other hand, if 
someone becomes too obsessed with their goals, they might ignore their health, 
relationships, or even lose sight of what really makes them happy. It can lead to, 
you know, a lot of stress and burnout, especially if they're constantly comparing 
themselves to others. So yeah, ambition can be great, but only if it's balanced with 
self-care and realistic expectations.
