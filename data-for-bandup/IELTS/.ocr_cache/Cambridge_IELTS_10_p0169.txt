Model and sample answers for Writing tasks
TEST A, WRITING TASK 1 (GENERAL TRAINING)
MODEL ANSWER
This model has been prepared by an examiner as an example of a very good answer.
However, please note that this is just one example out of many possible approaches.
Dear Sally,
My departure date for New Zealand is drawing near, and | am busy with
preparations. | have enrolled at an institution called ‘ABC English’ in Wellington,
for a six-month Advanced course, full-time. It is quite expensive but | am hopeful of
improving my pronunciation, especially.
To assist in covering the costs of my study, | aim to find a part-time job. Not only
are the college fees rather high, but also | know that rental accommodation in the
capital city will not be cheap, to say nothing of food and heating expenses! So |
would very much like to arrange some temporary employment before | arrive.
| was wondering if you could ask around among your friends and colleagues to see
if anyone wants a house-cleaner, gardener, or nanny for their children. | know you
have a wide social network in Wellington which you could canvas for me, if you
wouldn't mind. I'd be so grateful.
| look forward to seeing you soon.
Best wishes,
Margot
